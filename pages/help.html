How can we help?

A quick guide to using your portal.

How do I request a document?

Open Services, choose your document, and complete the request form. Your demo request will appear in My Request.

Where can I check my request?

Select My Request in the navigation to view requests created during this session.

How do I get help with official requirements?

Contact the RTU Pasig registrar for current requirements, processing times, and fees. The assistant provides general guidance only.
